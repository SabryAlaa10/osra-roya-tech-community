## Brand & Style

This design system channels an advanced, futuristic academic identity built specifically for computer science scholars and tech innovators. Blending **Dark Tech** and **Cyberpunk Academic Innovation**, the aesthetic merges high-precision technical interfaces with the depth and gravitas of higher education.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Forward-Looking & Intelligent:** Conveying mastery in software engineering, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.
- **Prestige & Excellence:** Deep slate-navy surfaces grounded by luminescent cyber accents, giving activities and achievements an elite, tournament-grade presence.
- **Community & Vitality:** Inspiring student participation through glowing interactive affordances, dynamic state responses, and polished telemetry aesthetics.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Ultra-Dark Oceanic Canvas:** Deep slate, void-navy, and obsidian tones creating deep atmospheric focus without pure harsh blacks.
- **Glassmorphic Substrates:** Translucent floating containers with sub-pixel borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`), linear-gradient backplates, and subtle backdrop blurs.
- **Neon Laser Edge Accents:** Precise, localized inner glow halos using electric teal, cyber cyan, and royal blue to indicate priority and active states.

## Colors

The color palette creates a luminous, sci-fi academic realm where dark matter backgrounds make high-frequency spectral hues project with high contrast and optical clarity.

### Brand Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#06B6D4` - Cyber Cyan):** Primary interactive elements, focused outlines, category badges, key branding highlights, and primary actionable states.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6` - Royal Blue):** Structural contrasts, secondary buttons, subtle atmospheric radial gradients, and academic hierarchy tags.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Neon Emerald):** Active membership statuses, technical tracks, verified badges, and real-time updates.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19` - Deep Navy/Slate Black):** Foundational canvas background. Creates depth when tiered against `#111827` (Card Surface) and `#1E293B` (Elevated Popovers/Navbars).

### Named Accent Colors
- **`achievement-gold` (`#F59E0B`):** Dedicated exclusively to competitive distinctions, hackathon victories, honors, and presidential crests.
- **`electric-teal` (`#14B8A6`):** Secondary accent for collaborative code sprints, team leader tags, and transitional gradients.
- **`border-subtle` & Glow Values:** Used to construct glassy edge-lit borders and cyber halos without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography fuses geometric engineering with high-legibility modern humanism. It is optimized for bilingual Arabic (RTL) and Latin (LTR) contexts.

### Font Hierarchy & Roles
- **Headlines & Technical Badges:** **Space Grotesk** (complemented in Arabic contexts by geometric modern sans with structured apertures such as Alexandria or Readex Pro). It lends a tech-forward, algorithmic voice to section titles, leader tags, and numeric metrics.
- **Body & Longform Descriptions:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (paired with balanced Arabic weights). Offers optical clarity, balanced proportions, and comfortable reading across multi-line bios and research abstracts.
- **Directional & RTL Harmony:** In RTL layouts, headlines retain strong structural weight (`700`), and line heights are expanded by 4–8px to accommodate Arabic ascenders and descenders seamlessly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on a balanced fluid grid anchored by rhythmic technical containers and expansive atmospheric breathing room.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page margins capped at `margin: 3rem` (max-width `1280px` centered). Cards span 3, 4, or 6 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1.25rem` and outer canvas margins of `2rem`. Member and activity cards adjust to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Member profiles and event listings collapse gracefully to a unified single column.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows a consistent 4px/8px mathematical base scale. Component gaps utilize `space-sm` for compact badge clusters, `space-md` for content-to-action margins, and `space-xl` for sectional separations. Directional margins invert symmetrically for RTL without layout disruption.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered frosted glass substrates, subtle edge lighting, and colored ambient glows rather than muddy black drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (L0):** Solid deep background `#0B0F19`, often enriched with low-opacity cyber-grid lines or sweeping ambient radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08) 0%, transparent 60%)`).
2. **Surface Layer (L1 - Cards & Lists):** Translucent fill `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a precise `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
3. **Elevated Layer (L2 - Hovered Elements & Menus):** Fill `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, a border of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`, and an ambient back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
4. **Accent Floating Layer (L3 - Modal Dialogs & Spotlight Banners):** Elevated glass frame augmented by an intense localized accent rim (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4), rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1))` along the border) with a diffused outer bloom `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (Level 2), projecting a modern, tactile, and engineered quality that avoids both sharp harshness and overly playful bubbles.

### Corner Radius Tokens
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form inputs, tooltips, tags, small buttons, and action chips.
- **Cards & Content Blocks (`1rem` / 16px):** Member cards, event containers, QR code plaques, and interactive modules.
- **Hero Windows & Outer Containers (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Major featured sections, spotlight banners, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Status indicators, committee tags, and social chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action Glow):** Gradient background from Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`) to Royal Blue (`#3B82F6`), dark navy bold typography (`#0B0F19`), `border-radius: 0.5rem`, and a soft hover glow `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary (Glass Cyber):** Translucent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`, text in Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`). On hover, background shifts to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Outline):** Transparent background with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border and white text, shifting to electric teal border on focus.

### Cards (Team & Event Cards)
- Structured on translucent dark slate (`#111827` at 80% opacity) backed by a 16px blur filter.
- Sub-pixel outer border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- On hover, transforms upward by `-4px`, with the border transitioning to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` and emitting an ambient teal/cyan soft underglow.
- Header profiles feature a subtle circular neon halo surrounding member avatars.

### Achievement Badges & Chips
- Status chips leverage pill styling with `font-family: Space Grotesk`, `label-sm` sizing, and uppercase/bold weight.
- **Achievement/Honor Items:** Bordered in `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)` with text color `#F59E0B`, accompanied by a micro radial glow.
- **Technical Track Chips:** Tinted neon emerald (`#10B981`) or cyber cyan backgrounds at 10% opacity with solid text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Dark inset surface (`#0B0F19`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Padding of `0.75rem 1rem`, placeholder text in muted steel-gray (`#64748B`).
- On active focus: border transitions to `#06B6D4`, casting an inner and outer cyan flare (`0 0 10px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Horizontal card items with distinct left/right accent indicators (depending on RTL/LTR).
- Alternating subtle hover states with soft backdrop blur.
- Category or timeline markers connected via a faint cyan laser vertical line (`1px dashed rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`).

### Directional & RTL Specifics
- In RTL, icons and action chevrons are automatically mirrored.
- Badge anchors and glowing achievement medals pin to the logical top-left or top-right based on document flow (`start` / `end`).
